Take back your privacy

Eraser sends data removal requests to 700+ data brokers on your behalf — the companies like Spokeo, BeenVerified and Whitepages that publish your address, phone number and relatives. Open source, free, and it runs on your own machine: requests go out from your mailbox, so no company ever holds your data. Incogni and DeleteMe charge $100+/year to do the same thing.

Download Broker directory

How it works

  1. Enter your details once (locally).
  2. Eraser emails every broker a GDPR / CCPA removal request.
  3. It watches your inbox for replies and shows what still needs a click.
  4. Re-run every 60–90 days — brokers re-list you.

Won't hand any tool your email password? Manual mode renders the emails for you to send by hand and still tracks them.
